# Genetics in this project

Ancient DNA has changed what we know about the past of Central and South Asia.
It can also be misused: to rank peoples, to claim "purity", or to settle
identity questions that DNA can't answer. This section explains what the
genetic evidence is, what it can and can't show, and how the project plans to
work with it.

**In Version 1, this section is documentation only.** No analysis is run, and
no personal genetic data is used, now or later.

## Reading order

1. [The Allen Ancient DNA Resource](aadr.md): the open dataset that most
   published studies of this region draw on.
2. [Reading the methods](methods.md): what PCA, f-statistics and qpAdm
   show, in plain language, and where each one misleads.
3. [Version 2 analysis plan](v2-analysis-plan.md): what we plan to do
   with open ancient-genome data, decided *before* looking at any results.

## Five things to keep in mind

1. **Ancestry is not identity.** DNA describes where someone's ancestors came
   from. It doesn't tell us what language they spoke, what they called
   themselves, or what culture they belonged to. Pashtun identity is defined by
   language, genealogy, custom and history, not by a genetic test.
2. **Everyone is mixed.** Every population in the region, ancient and modern,
   descends from several earlier groups. "Mixed" is the normal human condition,
   not a lesser one.
3. **Labels are tools.** Names like "Steppe_MLBA" or "BMAC" are labels for
   groups of sampled individuals. They're not peoples who called themselves that.
4. **Models are not facts.** A method like qpAdm says whether a model *fits*.
   Several different models often fit equally well.
5. **Samples are few.** Conclusions about millions of people often rest on a
   handful of individuals from a few sites. Always ask how many samples, from
   where.

## What we will never do

- Use personal or consumer DNA data (for example, 23andMe or AncestryDNA results).
- Make claims about any individual living person.
- Present genetic results as deciding who "really" is or isn't Pashtun.
- Use data in ways its terms of use don't allow.
