Where do the Pashtuns come from?

People have offered many answers: ancient peoples named by Greek historians, Scythian nomads, the lost tribes of Israel, Bronze Age migrants. This site lays out the evidence for each, in plain language, and is honest about how sure, or unsure, we can be.

How to read this site

1. What kind of evidence?

A coloured label says where an idea comes from: a DNA study, an ancient text, archaeology, a tradition or legend, and so on.

2. How sure can we be?

From well supported to disputed or not yet assessed, always judged on the evidence, not on how popular an idea is.

3. Has someone checked it?

Unverified means a person hasn't yet confirmed the sources say what we claim. Right now, everything is unverified. You can help.
