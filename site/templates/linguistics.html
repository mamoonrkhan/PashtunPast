Comparing Pashto with ancient Iranian languages

Cognates are words in different languages that come from the same ancestral word, like English father and German Vater. Each row below is one meaning, shown in Pashto and four ancient Iranian languages. Every form cites the dictionary it comes from.

Read with care. Shared words show that these languages are related. On their own they don't show which branch Pashto belongs to; that comes from shared sound changes, which the notes point out. Forms marked ? haven't yet been checked in the cited dictionary.
